# Recordings

The model is measured from these. They are not in the repo: download them next to it (Phase 0 will
add exact commands). Recorded audio that ships in the build (the onsets) must stay attributed here,
in the README and in the plugin/release notes.

| Source | Instrument | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Karoryfer × bigcat Cello](https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-karoryfer-x-bigcat-cello) (Karoryfer Samples, bigcat instruments) | cello | CC0 1.0 | **Primary cello.** 527 close-miked 24-bit / 44.1 kHz samples, every minor third, **no vibrato** (so the model's own vibrato is clean); harmonics slides and percussive noises. |
| [University of Iowa MIS](https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIScello2012.html) (Lawrence Fritts) | cello, violin | free, without restrictions | **Secondary.** Anechoic, every chromatic note, pp / mf / ff, per string, arco and pizzicato. Dry and complete: for separating the body from the string and filling dynamics. |
| [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) (Versilian Studios, Sam Gossner) | violin | CC0 1.0 (with a request that the samples not be sold) | **Primary violin.** Solo violin arco with vibrato, pizzicato, spiccato, tremolo. Some room in the recordings; vibrato has to be taken out in analysis. |

## Considered and not used

- **Philharmonia Orchestra samples:** may not be made available as samples or a sampler
  instrument; MP3 only.
- **Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra:** CC Sampling Plus 1.0 (restrictions on reuse).
- **Lewis E. Pyle Violin (Decent Samples):** download behind an email form; license not confirmed.
- **VCSL (Versilian):** no solo violin or cello (bowed psaltery only).
- **Karoryfer Vengeful / Merciful strings:** commercial.
